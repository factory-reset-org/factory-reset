# Animation Log (GV evidence, S4)

Every piece of motion on the agents, in the cutscenes and in their effects: how it moves, the numbers behind it, and what checks it. The design reasons are in the DesignDoc (agent animation, hits and shooting, hit effects and icons, cutscenes). The frame cost of all of it is measured in `OptimisationLog.md` (the S4 rows of 2026-10-10). Screenshots are in `Docs/Evidence/Agents/`.

Most of it is written in code on S3's frozen pivots rather than keyframed, for one reason: the motion has to follow something a clip cannot know in advance (the speed the body really moves at, the moment a hit lands, the shot a cutscene has reached). Where a clip does fit (the locomotion loops), the clips are generated from numbers, so a change is one edit and a rebuild.

## Locomotion

| Technique | How it moves | Numbers | Checked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Turning | The body turns towards where it walks with `Quaternion.RotateTowards`, about the vertical axis only, so it never snaps round and never tilts on a slope. Standing, it turns to the brain's look target instead | 360°/s; a waypoint counts as reached within 0.3 m | `AgentFacingTests` (a standing agent faces its look target within 3°; a walking one faces where it walks within 5°) |
| Speed easing | Speed eases towards the route's speed instead of jumping to it; a stop stays immediate so arrivals are exact | 12 m/s², 0 to 4.6 m/s in about 0.4 s | `AgentSchedulingTests.BodiesEaseUpToSpeed` (under 2 m/s after two frames, 4.6 ± 0.05 m/s after 0.6 s) |
| Blending into a new route | A replan that turns 25° to 120° from the current heading is joined with a quadratic Bézier B(t) = (1−t)²P + 2(1−t)tC + t²E: from the agent P to a point E about 0.35 s along the route, control point C half that distance ahead on the heading. The curve stays inside the triangle P, C, E, so it never overshoots | E is 0.6 to 1.6 m ahead; only while moving at 0.5 m/s or more; every segment passes the grid line check or the route is used as it is | `PathBlenderTests`; `AgentSchedulingTests.ARouteChangedMidWalkIsJoinedWithACurve` (keeps going 0.15 m or more the old way while bending, instead of pivoting) |
| No hitch on a replan | A new route that starts at the centre of the cell the agent is leaving skips that first waypoint when the agent is already nearer the second | Was a visible step back once in seven re-routes in a scripted run | `AgentFacingTests.ANewRouteDoesNotSendTheAgentBackToItsCellCentre` |
| Animation speed from real movement | The Blend Tree reads `GroundSpeed`: how far the body really moved this frame, capped at the speed it was told. Pressed against another body it shows standing, not running on the spot | 0.1 s damping in `AgentAnimatorBridge` so speed changes do not pop | `AgentSchedulingTests.ABodyPressedAgainstSomethingStandsInsteadOfRunningOnTheSpot` (commanded above 4 m/s, shown under 0.2 m/s) |
| Stand-off | Within reach of the player the body holds still and faces them instead of driving into the player's capsule; it walks on once the player is 0.3 m further away, so it does not flicker at the limit | 1.4 m Tracker, 1.6 Saboteur and Guard, 2.0 Captain | `AgentStandOffTests` |

## Clips generated from numbers

| Technique | How | Numbers | Checked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clip builder | Every locomotion clip is sine waves on the frozen pivots, `value = offset + amplitude · sin(2π(cycles · t/L + phase))`, or any looping curve where a sine will not do (the Captain's solved legs), written by `AgentAnimationBuilder` from `AgentMotionLibrary`. Whole cycles per clip make every loop seamless | 5 clips per agent (Idle, Walk, Run, LeanLeft, LeanRight) | `AgentAnimationAssetTests.EveryClipLoopsWithoutAJump`, `EveryCurveDrivesAPivotOfTheModel` |
| Blend Tree | One 2D Freeform Cartesian tree over Speed and TurnRate. Every clip keys the same channels, so blending never pulls a pivot towards a default | Walk / run at the brains' own speeds (Captain 2.5 / 4.6 m/s); leans at ±180°/s | `AgentAnimationAssetTests.ControllerBlendsFiveClipsOverSpeedAndTurnRate` |
| Captain legs, solved | `CaptainGait` plans each boot's path (planted: back under the body at the clip's speed; in the air: a Hermite arch that leaves and lands at ground speed; heel strike, roll, heel peel over the sole's tip) and solves the hip, knee and ankle with two-bone IK (`cos γ = (d² − a² − b²) / 2ab`, thigh 0.48 m, shin 0.45 m). The pelvis vaults, rolls and turns; the upper body counter-swings on sines | Walk 0.64 s at 2.5 m/s, 60% on the ground, 5 cm vault. Run 0.52 s at 4.6 m/s, 38% on the ground (both boots off between steps), crouched 12 cm. Legs keyed 72 times a cycle (run 108) | `CaptainLocomotionTests` on the built clips and the real model: no boot more than 5 mm into the floor, a touching vertex moves under 5 mm per 1/192 cycle, the swinging boot lifts over 15 cm (walk) and 30 cm (run) |
| Wheels | A wheel of radius r rolling d turns d / r radians, so each wheel turns `Speed · Δt / r` a frame. A clip could match only one speed and would slip at every other | Tracker's four wheels, the Saboteur's one | `AgentAnimationTests.OneTurnOfAWheelCoversItsCircumference` |
| Wind-up key | Turns at 180°/s times the Tracker's energy, so it slows as the toy runs down, and at −720°/s while it rewinds | Energy read from the brain through `IWindUpState` | `AgentAnimationTests.KeySlowsAsTheToyRunsDownAndSpinsBackWhileRewinding` |

## Fighting

| Technique | How | Numbers | Checked by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aim pose | An Aim override layer (cannon arm up, shield or torso braced) fades in while the agent attacks. Its weight is driven from code, so it never fights the locomotion layer | Fades over 0.1 s; the weapon turns the body to face the target and fires only within 25° | `AgentAnimationAssetTests.ShootersHaveAnAimOverrideLayerStartingAtWeightZero`, `AgentCombatTests.AShotWaitsForTheBodyToTurnToTheTargetSoItNeverLeavesSideways` |
| Barrels on target | `AgentCannonAim` turns each cannon arm at its shoulder, up-down and side to side, until the barrel points at the target, measured each frame on top of the clip and before the kick, weighted by the Aim layer. The shot leaves from the barrel's tip along its axis (the Captain's from its model's `Muzzle_Point`). Before, the Captain's barrels pointed 19° up while its shots went out level | Within 0.2° at full aim and about 5° while easing in and out (Captain, 6 m); the whole turn clamped at 35°; the Captain aims at 1.45 m so its beam comes at the camera | `AgentCannonAimTests` (3), `CaptainBodyTests.TheShotLeavesTheMuzzle…` |
| Captain aim pose | The cannon arm comes up level and forward, the chest turns 10° so the cannon side leads, the head turns back to the target and tips down, the left fist rises in a guard; only the upper body is keyed, so the legs keep walking | Built cannon direction (0.12 left, 0.05 up, 0.99 ahead), muzzle 2.04 m high, 1.56 m ahead | `CaptainLocomotionTests.TheAimPoseHoldsTheCannonLevelAndAhead` |
| Captain cannon | The power cell charges through the 0.3 s aim (glow up to 4×, flicker speeding up), the barrel creeps 3 cm out with a hum; the shot flashes white with sparks, smoke and a 0.08 s point light at the muzzle, the barrel slams 14 cm back along the forearm in 0.04 s and springs back on a damped spring; the spent cell drops to 15% and recharges | Spring settled by 0.4 s, cell recharged in 0.6 s; the cell goes dark with the visor | `CaptainBodyTests` (charge, flash, slam and settle, dark while dormant) |
| Shot recoil | Each shot kicks the arm holding that barrel up, the torso and the head back, added on top of the aimed pose in LateUpdate. On the Captain the kick flicks the cannon forearm (the elbow) | Arm 8° (was 28°, which pointed the barrel 47° up), torso 6°, head 8°, peaking at 15% of 0.28 s | `AgentCombatTests.EachShotKicksTheArmOfTheCannonThatFired`, `AgentCannonAimTests.TheShotLeavesFromTheBarrelsTipAndTheKickIsSmall` |
| Tracker pounce | The whole toy pounces: crouch and rock back, hop forward nose first with the bite, hop back. Keyframed as curves over normalised time and added on top of the Animator's pose, so nothing builds up | Every 1.3 s: wind-up 0.2 s, hop 0.35 m in 0.15 s, back in 0.25 s | `AgentMeleeDamageTests.TheTrackersPounceIsCosmeticUntilItIsGivenDamage` |
| Saboteur swipe | Arm up with the pincer open while the body twists away, then twists back, leans in, rolls forward and slashes, alternating arms. Re-tuned for the remade model's hanging arms (17 Oct): the strike ends at chest height in front, not behind the hip | Every 1.2 s; shoulder -140° then -105°, elbow -30° then 60°; rolls 0.5 m on its wheel (was 0.3, which closed 17 cm short of a player 2 m away); the damage lands at contact (half way), not at the start, so the wind-up is a telegraph | `AgentMeleeDamageTests.EachSwipeHurtsThePlayerOnceAtTheMomentOfContact`, `APlayerWhoBacksOffDuringTheWindUpIsNotHit` |
| Saboteur hands-on | When it closes a door, arms a trap or takes a battery: turns to the target, leans in, both arms reach forward and down, the pincers open and snap shut, then it straightens up; a stolen battery appears in the crate on its back at the pinch | 0.7 s; lean 18°, shoulders -70°, elbows 35°, turn up to 60°; rolls in by however far the target is beyond the pincers' 1.3 m reach (up to 0.6 m); the action itself stays instant | `AgentSabotageTests.TheSaboteurReachesInToActAndAStolenBatteryShowsInItsCrate` |
| Tracker excitement | A happy puppy while it is Distracted by a thrown toy: a hop when it spots the toy, then while it watches a bounce, a fast tail wag, perked ears flapping out of step, a head tilt and the key at 2×; while it follows a walking toy, only the wag and the ears. Layered after the pounce in LateUpdate, eased in and out | Spotted: hop 0.12 m, ears +25°, head up 10° in 0.4 s. Watching: bounce 0.04 m at 3 Hz, wag ±30° at 6 Hz, ears 20° ± 10° at 3 Hz, head tilt ±15° at 0.7 Hz. Blend 0.2 s | `TrackerExcitementTests` (2): offsets present and back to rest within 0.25 s; cut at once when knocked out |

## Taking hits

| Technique | How | Numbers | Checked by / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hit squash | Each hit squashes the model and throws a few sparks at the chest; no word, so a burst of hits does not fill the screen | 0.15 s | `AgentKnockdownTests.AHitThrowsSparksAndSquashesButShowsNoWord` |
| Knock-out | Explosion burst, a comic word ("KRZZT!"), the body slumps like a powered-down toy (the Captain kneels), lies until its reboot, then gets back up. A body without a pose still tips over | Tracker 7 s, Guard 8 s, Captain 6 s down | `AgentKnockdownTests.KnockOutExplodesTipsOverGoesDarkAndGetsBackUp`; `Evidence/Agents/captain_knockout_word.png` |
| Toy slumps | Each robot powers down its own way. Tracker: the key unwinds, the body tips forward and sinks, head, ears and tail droop; it winds back up in ticks, pops its ears and shakes. Saboteur: wobbles on its wheel, then leans with arms hanging and pincers open; straightens and snaps them shut. Guard: slumps at the waist, cannon down, smoke from the head; straightens in three servo steps and swings the cannon up | Tracker 15° and 5 cm, key −1080°/s dying over 1.6 s, 4 ticks of 90°, shake ±10°. Saboteur wobble ±12°, lean 30°, claws 1.6 cm off the floor. Guard waist 25°, head 30°, cannon 35°. Down 0.5 / 0.95 / 0.6 s, up 1.3 / 0.9 / 1.1 s | `AgentSlumpTests.KnockedOutEachRobotSlumpsThenIsBackAtRestAfterTheReboot` (3 robots, real prefabs); `Evidence/Agents/agent_slumps.png` |
| Slumps at a wall | The forward lean shrinks to fit the room in front, measured once with two sphere casts as it goes down, against each model's measured reach standing and slumped | Guard 0.53 → 1.13 m, Saboteur 0.5 → 0.93 m, Captain 0.5 → 0.9 m (its front boot; only the bow shrinks). | Dizzy stars | Three comic stars circle above the head while knocked out, popping in after the explosion and shrinking away at the reboot; never on a scrap | 0.35 s delay, one turn per 1.1 s, ring radius 0.3 m, stars 0.2 m | `AgentSlumpTests.StarsShowOnlyWhileDownAndNeverOnAScrap` |
| Captain kneel | A 3.25 m boss toppling like a toy looked wrong, so it drops onto its right knee, fist on the front knee, cannon planted muzzle-down. The fist pose was the only one, of over 100,000 searched, whose arm clears the torso | Drop 0.492 m in 0.55 s, landing a little heavy; contacts within 6 mm of the floor | `CaptainBodyTests.TheDormantCaptainKneelsOnTheFloorWhereItWasMeasured` (real body), `AgentKnockdownTests.TheCaptainKneelsInsteadOfTippingThenStepsBackUp` |

| Scrapped (Saboteurs) | The same burst with "SCRAPPED!", lies there, then sinks into the floor and shrinks with a last puff | Lies 1.2 s, sinks over 0.7 s | `AgentKnockdownTests.ScrappedAgentShowsItsWordSinksAndIsSwitchedOff`, `AgentCombatTests.SaboteurIsScrappedForGoodWhenItGoesDown` |

## Frame cost

Measured in the Editor from Bootstrap (2026-10-10, ProfilerRecorder, about 3,000 frames each). Full tables and method in `OptimisationLog.md`.

| | Chapter 4 play, all 7 agents | Agents switched off | Cutscene (ch3) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Game loop, mean / p99 | 7.36 / 11.12 ms | 6.24 / 11.45 ms | 7.25 / 12.43 ms |
| Animators (all) | 0.144 ms | 0 | 0.147 ms |
| Scripts (Update + LateUpdate) | 0.694 ms | 0.329 ms | 0.509 ms |
| Timeline (`Director.PrepareFrame` + `ProcessFrame`) | - | - | 0.327 ms |

All of the agents' motion together costs about 1.1 ms of a 16.7 ms frame (60 fps), and the game stays under 12.5 ms at p99 in play and in the cutscene.

**Limits:** Editor timings with Mono, one machine, one session each; the screenshots are at the Game view's size.
