## IS: Guard Bot (tactical A* + cover scoring)

| Topic | What to say | Open |

| Cover score | Weighted sum of protection (full 1, half 0.5), range fit to the ideal distance, travel cost and peek ability; top 3 by cheap octile cost, then true tactical A* cost, then the best is reserved | `AI/Agents/Guard/CoverEvaluator.cs` |

## Likely questions

- **Why A* and not Dijkstra for the Guard?** It needs one route to one cover at a time. A* with an admissible heuristic gives the same optimal cost with a fraction of the work (19% of the cells with the plain cost, 42% with the tactical cost). The Captain uses Dijkstra fields because it asks about many goals at once.
- **Is the tactical heuristic still admissible?** Yes: the heuristic is octile distance, and every step costs at least its octile length (hidden steps exactly, exposed steps four times), so it never overestimates.
- **What stops two agents taking the same cover?** Cell reservations on the blackboard: a reserved cell is not on offer to anyone else, and is released on stun, death or a new choice.
- **Why do props never allocate per frame?** Pools for effects, `OverlapBoxNonAlloc` with a reused buffer for the plate, cached sight checks; the plate measured 0 bytes over 20 deliveries.
- **How is the brain tested without Unity?** `GuardBrainTests` build a `GridGraph`, a fake visibility and a blackboard, and tick the brain with chosen times: every state change and edge case runs in milliseconds in EditMode.
