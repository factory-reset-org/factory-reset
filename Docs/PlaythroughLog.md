## Checks to tick during the run

- [ ] Blaster: shots, sparks, reload, dry click, overcharge pickup
- [ ] Wind-up toy: throw, WIND!, ticking, the Tracker goes to it, toy count on the HUD
- [ ] Pushable boxes: push, settle, plate latches, noise
- [ ] Doors: open, close, nothing visible above a closed door
- [ ] Traps and charger: damage, refill
- [ ] Hit marker on the crosshair when a shot lands
- [ ] M mutes and unmutes
- [ ] Pause, resume, cutscenes: agents freeze and carry on
- [ ] Death and Play again, then Quit
- [ ] No red errors in the player log (`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\<Company>\<Product>\Player.log`)
